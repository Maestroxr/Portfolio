resources and of the numbers of heroes and creatures (stat_attack, stat_defense, stat_power, stat_knowledge, damage,
health, speed, movement, mana, morale, luck, experience) in color with an outline (UI/Generated/Icons), and draws the
"fly" pointer from angel-wings (spell_bless).
