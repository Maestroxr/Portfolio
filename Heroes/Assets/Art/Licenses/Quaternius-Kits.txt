Quaternius kits (quaternius.com), CC0

The textured kits the remade Grim Realm is built from, the free Standard versions downloaded from
https://quaternius.itch.io on 2026-10-07. Every one of them is CC0 1.0 Universal (the licence files that came with
them are in Art/Models/Quaternius/Kits).

- Universal Base Characters [Standard], https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
  Used (Kits/Characters): Superhero_Male_FullBody, Superhero_Female_FullBody (their heads, eyes and brows are put on
  the outfits; the upper body of the male is the brawler's), and the hairstyles rigged to the head bone (Hair).
- Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy [Standard], https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html
  Used (Kits/Characters): the Peasant and Ranger outfits of both sexes, and their legs and feet on their own (Parts).
- Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2 [Standard],
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html, https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html
  Used (Kits/Animation): UAL1_Standard and UAL2_Standard (without root motion); the builder copies the clips the people
  play into legacy clips of the theme.
- Medieval Village MegaKit [Standard], https://quaternius.com/packs/medievalvillagemegakit.html
  Used (Kits/Village): every modular piece, for the towns and buildings.
- Fantasy Props MegaKit [Standard], https://quaternius.com/packs/fantasypropsmegakit.html
  Used (Kits/Props): every prop but Chest_Wood (10 MB of geometry).
- Stylized Nature MegaKit [Standard], https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html
  Used (Kits/Nature): every model (the FBX (Unity) export).

The textures were prepared once when they were downloaded (scratchpad tool prep_kits.py of the session that added
them): base colours scaled down to at most 2048 pixels (JPEG when they have no alpha), normal maps in Unity's
convention, and each material's ORM or roughness map repacked as one map for URP Lit (metallic in red, occlusion in
green, smoothness in alpha). Materials.json in each kit says which textures each material of its models wears.
