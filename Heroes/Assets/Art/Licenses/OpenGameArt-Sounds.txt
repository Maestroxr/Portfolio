Sound effects from OpenGameArt.org

Source: https://opengameart.org

         always credited them; see below)

Used (Art/Audio/Sfx):
- the sounds of the spells in a battle: Curse.wav, Fireball.wav, Heal.wav, Lightning.wav, Spell.wav
- the sound of a spell that helps a stack: Buff.ogg
- the jingles of a battle's start, a victory, a defeat, a new level and a new week: BattleStart.ogg, Victory.ogg,
  Defeat.ogg, LevelUp.ogg, NewWeek.ogg

These eleven came with the first version of the game, whose README credits its sounds to OpenGameArt and Kenney
(CC0), and nothing in them names Kenney (see Kenney-License.txt for the ones that do). The page each one was taken
from was not recorded. The five .wav files and the five jingles say only that they were made with FL Studio 11 (the .ogg files
carry a track number as their title); Buff.ogg carries no tags at all. Until those pages are found and listed here (or
the sounds are replaced by ones whose source is known), their licence rests on that note alone.
